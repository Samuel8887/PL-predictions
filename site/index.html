Behind the forecast Method & limitations

Match estimates use recency-weighted scoring histories with shrinkage towards league averages. Outcome calibration is selected on earlier fixtures, then checked on a chronological holdout. These are statistical estimates, not guarantees.

Player projections are heuristics, not confirmed team sheets. They do not incorporate future transfers or tactical changes. Season simulations hold team strength fixed and share exact ties after points, goal difference and goals scored; head-to-head and playoffs are not modelled.
